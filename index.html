Foundations

  • Study design, data types & variables in clinical research

Inferential Statistics in SPSS

  • t-tests, ANOVA, chi-square & non-parametric tests
  • Correlation, linear & logistic regression
  • Survival analysis & Kaplan–Meier curves

Advanced

  • Multivariable models & adjustment for confounders
  • Diagnostic accuracy — sensitivity, specificity, ROC/AUC
  • Reporting analyses to reviewer-ready standard

  • Data cleaning, joins & feature engineering
  • Visualisation with Matplotlib, Seaborn & Plotly
  • Notebook workflow & reproducible research

  • The ML pipeline for clinicians — train, validate, deploy
  • Classification & regression on real clinical outcomes
  • Model evaluation — AUC, calibration, decision curves

  • Ship your first AI-powered clinical prediction model
